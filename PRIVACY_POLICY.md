# Deino Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 October 2026*

Deino is a meeting notepad that runs on your Mac. It is built so that your
meetings stay under your control. This policy explains what Deino does with
your data.

## Summary

- Deino has **no servers** and **no accounts**. There is no Deino cloud.
- Deino collects **no telemetry, analytics or crash reports**.
- **Audio and transcripts never leave your Mac.** Recording and transcription
  run locally.
- Data leaves your Mac only when **you** connect a service, and then it goes
  **directly** from the app to that service.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Audio recordings** of your microphone and system audio.
- **Transcripts**, produced by speech-recognition models that run on your Mac.
- **Notes, summaries and meeting metadata**, stored in a local database in the
  app's data folder.
- **Settings and downloaded models**.

You can delete any meeting, or the whole app data folder, at any time.

## What may leave your Mac, and only if you set it up

- **Your chosen AI provider** (for example the Claude API or the OpenAI API)
  receives the transcript text and notes needed to generate summaries or answer
  your questions. That provider's own privacy policy and data-retention terms
  apply. If you use the built-in local model or a local server such as Ollama,
  nothing is sent over the internet for this step.
- **Integrations you connect** (for example calendars, Slack, Notion, email or
  task tools) receive only the content you choose to send them, such as notes
  or action items. Each integration's own privacy policy applies.
- **Model downloads.** When you download a transcription or local AI model,
  Deino fetches the files from their public host (HuggingFace). These requests
  contain no meeting data.

Deino never sends audio anywhere.

## Updates

Automatic update checks are turned off. Deino does not contact an update
server; new versions are installed by hand.

## Children

Deino is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

## Changes

If Deino's data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the change
ships, with a new "Last updated" date.
